The project

C29 · AI Immersion Task 01 — Design Thinking for an AI-powered early-detection system for Red Palm Weevil on date palms. By Naveenprasanth M (B.E CSE, Cyber Security).

Phase 01 · Empathise

Field visit to a date palm farm, Rathinam Technical Campus, Coimbatore. Three observations: a healthy 3-year-old palm crown, damaged shoots with weevil-associated holes, and the weekly manual inspection routine (~10 min per plant).

Phase 02 · Define

Problem: farmers rely on fixed weekly visual inspection, so Red Palm Weevil damage progresses undetected for 2–3 days each cycle — the treatment protects for 4–5 days, shorter than the 7-day check interval. Every current method is scheduled, not signal-driven.

Objectives

  1. Screen suspected infestation within 48 hours.
  2. Enable faster inspection and response after an AI warning.
  3. Create a digital inspection record for all 110 palms.
  4. Prototype a low-cost mobile computer-vision detection system.
Phase 03 · Ideate

Winner — 4.45/5: AI-powered mobile computer-vision detection. A farmer photos the palm crown; a CV model analyses it for holes, damaged shoots and abnormal crown areas; the app returns a Low / Medium / High risk result and stores the inspection history.

Runner-up (4.15/5): fixed AI camera monitoring — lost on hardware cost and scaling to 110 palms vs. a phone used during the routine.

Phase 04 · Deliver

Before

  • Manual inspection, no digital AI screening
  • Delayed recognition between rounds
  • Signs assessed by human eye only
  • Little structured history

After

  • AI-assisted screening during inspection
  • Immediate Low / Med / High result
  • Early warning on visible indicators
  • Digital history by palm / zone

Feasibility: student-team buildable as a mobile/web prototype + trained CV model. Cost: low — smartphone camera + software. Risk: false positives — farmer verifies before treatment, model improves with validated images.

Outcome: all three testers rated the app "very good". The camera capture issue they reported was traced to blocked live-camera access over plain http:// and a shutter that stayed disabled without motion sensors — both fixed (photo-picker fallback + sensor-less shutter). Full record: VALIDATION.md in this repository.

Phase 07 · Conclusion
  • Real field data beats assumed problems.
  • AI sharpens root-cause thinking.
  • A scoring matrix forces honest trade-offs.
  • A simple, testable AI beats complex hardware for a student prototype.
